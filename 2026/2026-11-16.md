## 01:58 Community News

### 02:01 CircuitPython 11.0.0-alpha.3 is Released

The third alpha of CircuitPython 11 adds display mirroring over the web workflow, the start of a video pipeline on the ESP32-P4, audio PWM output on the ESP32-S2 and ESP32-S3, and better MIDI file playback, along with many speedups in graphics and JPEG decoding. Libraries from the 10.x bundles still work with 11.0.0 builds, but check the release notes for incompatible changes before upgrading \- [Adafruit Blog](https://blog.adafruit.com/2026/11/12/circuitpython-11-0-0-alpha-3-released/) and [release notes](https://github.com/adafruit/circuitpython/releases/tag/11.0.0-alpha.3).

### 02:36 In Memoriam: Tim Peters, 1952-2026

Guido van Rossum announced that Tim Peters, one of Python's earliest and most influential contributors, died on July 15\. Tim Peters wrote the Zen of Python (PEP 20\) and designed Timsort, the sorting algorithm for Python lists later adopted by Java and Android, and was known on the Python mailing lists as the "Timbot" for careful, patient answers to user questions \- [Discussions on Python.org](http://Python.org) and [Adafruit Blog](https://blog.adafruit.com/2026/11/11/tim-peters-python-user-2-and-prolific-contributor-has-died/).

### 03:10 Project of the Week: An Ambient Daylight Clock

Hannah Ilea built a 24-hour analog clock whose ring of 48 NeoPixels lights the span of daylight for a chosen place, plus a control box with a knob, a slider and toggles for changing the date, latitude and longitude. An Adafruit Feather RP2040 runs it in CircuitPython with asyncio \- [hannahilea.com](http://hannahilea.com) and [GitHub](https://github.com/hannahilea/notebook-playground/tree/main/daylight-clock).
